How to Play
  1. Click a line between two dots to draw it.
  2. If your line completes a box (all 4 sides), you'll get a civics question.
  3. Answer correctly to claim the box and get another turn!
  4. Answer incorrectly and Senator Square claims the box.
  5. The player with the most boxes when the board is full wins!
  6. Every 30 seconds, a bonus question pops up automatically — stay sharp!

You have 15 seconds per question. Good luck, citizen!
